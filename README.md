# Telecom Customer Segmentation 📊

## Business Objective
In the highly competitive telecom industry, understanding customer behavior is the key to retention and growth. This project utilizes machine learning to divide a customer base into distinct demographic and financial profiles, allowing marketing teams to target specific groups effectively and maximize Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

## Project Overview
* **Algorithm Used:** K-Means Clustering (Unsupervised Learning)
* **Optimization:** The Elbow Method was utilized to determine the optimal number of clusters (K=3).
* **Tools & Libraries:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly.

## Identified Customer Segments
Based on historical data, the algorithm identified three distinct customer archetypes:
1. **The VIP Wealthy:** High income, long employment history, and premium value.
2. **The Established Professionals:** Stable middle-class income, low default risk, and highly reliable.
3. **The Young & Developing:** Younger demographic with lower current income but high future potential.

## Actionable Marketing Insights
* **Premium Packages:** Target the *VIP Wealthy* with high-speed international roaming and white-glove services.
* **Loyalty Contracts:** Target *Established Professionals* with stable, 24-month family bundle discounts.
* **Flexible Plans:** Offer prepaid or low-cost flexible plans to the *Young & Developing* segment to build brand loyalty as their careers grow.
